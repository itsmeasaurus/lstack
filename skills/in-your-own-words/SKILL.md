name: in-your-own-words
description: Restate in your own words what you think the user's goal is and what problem they're trying to solve.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Restate, in your own words and in a few sentences:

- What you think the user's goal is.
- What problem they are trying to solve.
- Any assumption you are making that they should confirm.

Don't start the work. Wait for them to confirm or correct you.
